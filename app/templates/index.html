THE APPROACH

Two signals. One research framework.

01 ◉

Facial representation

Video frames are processed to extract facial features using a deep-learning feature extractor.

02 ∿

Speech representation

Audio is extracted from the video and represented using Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCCs).

03 ✳

Feature fusion

The two feature representations are combined by a trained model to produce an experimental class prediction.

i

An important note about this research

MindScope is an experimental research prototype, not a medical device or diagnostic tool. Its predictions may be inaccurate and must not be used to diagnose depression or guide treatment. A qualified mental health professional can provide appropriate assessment and support.
